Colors

Color doesn't cause a mood, but it's a strangely reliable way of describing one. The quiz on the Discover page matches four of these to a mood and flower.

Red

Red carries urgency and warmth in equal measure. It's the color of both passion and warning an intensity that's hard to ignore. Here it belongs to feelings that ask to be felt fully rather than managed.

Blue

Blue is often linked to depth and steadiness the color of open sky and quiet water. It suggests a mind that has settled, even briefly, into stillness.

Yellow

Yellow is the color least likely to sit still. It's associated with optimism and lightness the visual equivalent of a good mood that hasn't been examined too closely, just enjoyed.

Purple

Purple sits between red's intensity and blue's calm, which fits a color long linked to creativity and introspection. It suggests a mind working quietly in the background.

Green

Green is the color most associated with growth and recovery the color of things renewing themselves without being rushed. It tends to show up in a mood that's slowly repairing rather than reacting.

Orange

Orange takes red's warmth and drops the intensity, leaving something more sociable than urgent. It's associated with enthusiasm that hasn't tipped into overwhelm yet.

About

SoulPalette started as a simple question: does the color you're drawn to on a given day actually say something about how you feel? There's no real science behind the pairing moods, colors, and flowers here are connected by association and feeling, not research.

What it's meant to be is small and low-stakes: four moods, four colors, four flowers, and a short quiz that connects them. The Journal keeps a quiet record of your results over time, stored only in your browser nothing is sent anywhere or shared with anyone.

Take what's useful from it, and leave the rest.

A quiet way to notice how you feel

A mood settles in, and with it comes a color you're drawn to without quite knowing why the yellow of an easy morning, the deep red of something urgent in the chest. SoulPalette is a small, unscientific space for following that pattern: the feeling, the color it resembles, and the flower whose character seems to match. Nothing to score, nothing to fix just a gentle pause to notice yourself a little more clearly today.
